Cardinality vs selectivity

Two different measurements
TermMeaningExample
CardinalityNumber of distinct values10,000 distinct payers
Selectivity (match fraction)Matching rows / total rows30 / 300,000 = 0.01%
Low cardinalityFew distinct valuesAPP and WEB channels
High match fractionMost rows qualifypayer_account <> one payer

Remember

Lower match fraction often makes index access more competitive; it is not a guarantee.

B-tree versus BRIN

Precise keys versus compact page-range summaries

B-tree

Stores searchable keys and row references. Good for selective lookup and ordering.

BRIN

Summarizes ranges of heap pages. Very compact when value order correlates with physical order.

→
